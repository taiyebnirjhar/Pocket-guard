Pocket Guard Privacy Policy

App: Pocket Guard (com.taiyeb.pocketguard) · Developer: Taiyeb · Last updated: October 2026

Pocket Guard is an anti-theft and personal-safety app. It works on your phone: it has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send any of your data to the developer or to anyone else. This policy explains what the app uses on your phone and why.

What the app uses, and why

  • Motion, proximity and light sensors: to tell when the phone is taken out of a pocket or picked up from a table while the guard is armed. Readings are processed on the phone as they arrive and are not stored, except a short detection log you can view and clear in Settings.
  • Camera (photos): when the theft siren starts, to take a few photos of whoever holds the phone. They are saved on the phone, in History.
  • Camera and microphone (video with sound): only after you start SOS, until you unlock, to record what happens. Recordings are saved on the phone, in History. They leave the phone only if you share them yourself.
  • Accessibility service (optional): only to detect volume + and − held together for the SOS gesture. It does not read the screen, what you type, or any other content, and nothing it sees leaves the phone.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): the names of your paired earbuds or watch, so you can choose which ones warn you when they lose the phone. Stored on the phone.
  • Physical activity: to remind you to arm the guard on a bus or train. Not stored.
  • Notifications, Do Not Disturb access and alarm over the lock screen: to show the guard's status and to make the alarm heard and seen.
  • Text-to-speech: the spoken call for help uses your phone's own voice engine.

Sharing

The app shares nothing by itself. When you choose to share a recording or photo from History, it goes only to the app or person you pick. Personal data is never sold.

Storage, retention and deletion

Everything stays in the app's private storage on your phone. History keeps warnings for 7 days and alarms for 90 days, then deletes them with their photos and recordings. You can clear History at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Security

Data is kept in storage only the app can read. Stopping an alarm needs your phone's own unlock (PIN, pattern, password or biometrics).

Children

Pocket Guard is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example sharing your location with emergency contacts), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent before using them.
